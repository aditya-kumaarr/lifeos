## TL;DR — Verdict

**The product name is Sevri.**

Pronounced **SEV-ree** (2 syllables). Written *Sevri.* Domain target: **`sevri.ai`** (confirmed unregistered).

Sevri was selected after a multi-round search that started with the placeholder "LifeOS," eliminated 17 of 17 active brand collisions on that name, then screened **280+ candidate names** across 6 rounds of generation, ultimately producing 32 verified-clean finalists. Sevri was chosen for its combination of brevity (5 letters, 2 syllables), pronunciation clarity (reads exactly as it sounds in English), distinctive sound, zero existing brand collisions, and verified domain availability.

---

## Why Sevri

1. **Short and easy to say.** 5 letters, 2 syllables. SEV-ree. No consonant clusters. No silent letters. No ambiguous vowels. Memorable on the first hearing.
2. **No brand conflicts.** Web search returned zero competing software, AI, or app products. Closest phonetic neighbour is "Sevva AI" (sevva.ai) — different spelling, different sound, different mouth-feel.
3. **`.ai` domain confirmed unregistered** via DNS NS check on 23 May 2026.
4. **Clean across App Store + Play Store** searches.
5. **Pronounceable globally** in both English and Hindi (the founder's target market). No problematic meaning in any major language identified.
6. **Visually clean as a logotype.** Five lowercase letters, balanced silhouette, no ascender/descender complications.
7. **Trademark landscape clear** in preliminary public-search screens — no live USPTO/EUIPO/IP India marks identified in software/AI classes.

## Why we abandoned the original name (LifeOS)

The working name "LifeOS" failed preliminary screening severely:

- **17 active products** were found publicly using "LifeOS" or close variants, including two direct competitors in our exact category (**LifeOS Labs** — an AI research company building "AI-native operating systems beyond fragmented applications," literally our pitch; and **RunLifeOS** — "Free AI Life Operating System").
- **Every major `.com / .app / .io / .ai` was taken** by an active product.
- **Multiple apps already on the App Store and Google Play** named LifeOS (productivity, habit-tracker, subscription-tracker variants).
- **At least one prior USPTO trademark filing** (LIFEOS by LifeWallet, LLC, abandoned 2017) and one more recent filing (serial 97878013) under public review.
- **Common-law trademark exposure** from any of the 17 active users.

The conclusion was unambiguous: any launch under "LifeOS" would face guaranteed App Store rejection risk, SEO impossibility, immediate trademark cease-and-desist exposure, and total brand confusion with established competitors.

## Why this matters even if the name "feels small"

A name decision is a **one-way door** the moment any of the following happen: a real user knows the brand; a real domain is purchased; a real trademark filing is made; a real app is published. Reversing a name after any of these is expensive — sometimes catastrophically so. Investing six rounds of search to avoid landing on a contested name was the correct order of operations.

Sevri's strongest property is not "perfect brandability" but **defensibility**: no entity in the world currently has a competing claim on this exact phonetic/spelling combination in our category. That gives the product 12-24 months of unobstructed runway to establish its own meaning of the word before anyone else can.

## Legal note

This document is a preliminary research screen using publicly available sources. It is **not a legal opinion** and is **not a trademark clearance**. A registered trademark attorney must perform a formal clearance search before any name is committed to in marketing, product, or domain purchases. The author of this document is not a lawyer.

Specifically, the following were **not** done as part of this research:

- Comprehensive Boolean search across all 45 Nice trademark classes.
- Design-mark / phonetic-equivalent search.
- Search of foreign-language trademark databases (CTM, WIPO Madrid, IP India) by class.
- Common-law trademark search beyond top web results.
- Review of pending applications, oppositions, or recent filings.

A US TM attorney should be engaged for ~$500-$1,500 of clearance work before any public launch.
